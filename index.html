The process

Four stages to a
mirror finish

01 / 04

Assess & wash

Every vehicle is inspected and safely pre-washed before any polish or pad touches the paint.

02 / 04

Decontaminate

Clay and iron removers lift bonded contamination a wash alone can never shift.

03 / 04

Correct & polish

Machine polishing levels the surface, removing swirls, light scratches and haze.

04 / 04

Protect & seal

A ceramic or sealant layer locks in the finish and keeps future maintenance simple.
